Solana Mobile Clock In

AskLens Guard

Tap Share on any link or wallet address. Get a green, amber or red answer.

An Android app for Solana Seeker owners. Solo build by Adel, Nigeria.

The problem

On a phone, you copy things you can't check.

A link in a group chatIt says "claim your airdrop". Maybe it's a fake dApp Store page.
An address from a strangerLooks right at the start and end. Address poisoning counts on that.
No pause built inCopy, paste, sign. The check, if any, happens after the money moves.
What it does

Share it to AskLens Guard and see if it's safe before you open or pay.

It lives in the Android share menu, so it works from Telegram, X and the browser without copying anything around.

How it works

Share menu to verdict, in a few seconds.

1SharePick AskLens Guard in the share sheet.
2Live checkLinks go to three live Telegraph checkers at once.
3Wallet factsSolana addresses get a profile: balance, age, activity, tokens.
4ProofSave a signed receipt of the check on Solana.

The Solana part

Connect your wallet. Keep a receipt you can show anyone.

  • Connect with Mobile Wallet Adapter, the same way Seeker apps do.
  • Wallet profile read from the Solana network: balance, age, transaction count, top tokens.
  • Save proof sends a small memo transaction: the verdict, a short fingerprint of what you checked, and a time. Open it in the explorer.

Why mobile

The risky moment happens in a chat app, on a phone.

Android share sheetThe check sits one tap from where the link arrives. No copy, no switching apps.
Wallet connectMobile Wallet Adapter signs the receipt in the wallet app you already use.
Seeker ownersSelf-custody phone, dApp Store, constant exposure to new links and addresses.

Honest status

What's real today, and what isn't.

Real
  • Share a link in and get a verdict from the live service
  • Ethereum 0x address checks (slower, often "not sure")
  • History, streak, share card
  • Solana wallet profile from the Solana network
  • App compiles and bundles for Android
Limits
  • The live service doesn't check Solana addresses. We show wallet facts, not a scam verdict, and the screen says so.
  • About 10 checks per 10 minutes per phone
  • Proof receipt: code written, devnet send not yet proven end to end
  • Not yet tested on a physical phone at time of writing
